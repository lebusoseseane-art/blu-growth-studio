# Tru Blu Growth

I am rebuilding the TRU BLU Digital Marketing website.

TRU BLU is a premium full-stack digital marketing and growth agency based in Gauteng, RSA.

The core positioning is:

“WE DON'T JUST BUILD THE BRAND. WE CEMENT THE BUSINESS.”

Supporting philosophy:

“Give your business a strong brand. Build a strong business for your brand to represent.”

TRU BLU provides:

1. Branding Solutions

2. Social media management

3. Content creation & strategy

4. Meta advertising

5. TikTok advertising

6. Reels / short-form content.   7. SEO, google ads and search marketing

8. PR campaigns

9. Events and activations

10. Business growth consultations

11. Digital marketing strategy

12. Growth strategy

Our specialty is:

META ADS + TIKTOK ADS + REELS.

We are a full-stack digital agency, but social advertising and short-form content are our strongest differentiators.

IMPORTANT CREATIVE DIRECTION:

The existing design is too futuristic, too AI-oriented and too much like a technology/SaaS company.

Do NOT create a cyberpunk, neon, AI-startup or futuristic aesthetic.

TRU BLU should feel like:

SANTORINI × LUXURY EDITORIAL × MODERN BUSINESS × DIGITAL PERFORMANCE.

Deep Mediterranean navy

Aegean blue

Warm architectural white

DESIGN LANGUAGE:

Use large editorial typography.

Use generous whitespace.

Use asymmetric layouts.

Use full-bleed photography.

Use premium magazine/editorial composition.

Use subtle micro-interactions.

Use restrained glass and chrome elements.

Use elegant cards rather than generic SaaS cards.

Use photography as a major part of the identity.

The website should feel like a luxury fashion brand that happens to be an elite digital growth agency.
